I know who you are

You have an adult child who has pulled away. Maybe it has been a few months. Maybe it has been twenty years. Maybe there was a big fight, and maybe there wasn't one at all, and you cannot say when it started. You still look at your phone more than you would like anybody to know. You still set a place at the table, if only in your heart.

I know that ache because I carried it too. I carried it a long time, longer than I like to say, and I carried it wrong. I thought it was my job to bring my son around. I called, I quoted Scripture, and I fussed. All I did was push him farther off.

It was only when I quit trying to do God's work that I found peace, and I found it before my boy ever picked up the phone.

TWO THINGS, AND THE BOOK IS ABOUT BOTH

Three things this book is not

It is not telling you that you are a bad parent.

You are not. You hurt this much because you love this much.

It is not asking you to say your child's choices are fine.

You can keep every one of your beliefs. Peace and approval are two different things.

And it is not a promise.

I cannot tell you your child is coming home. I can tell you that the Lord is near to the brokenhearted, and I have seen Him keep that promise more times than I can count.

Who is writing to you

My name is Evelyn Millers. I am eighty-six years old, and I was a nurse for thirty years. I am not a preacher, and I never went to a school to learn about families. Everything I am going to tell you, I learned at a kitchen table, at a hospital bed, or on my knees.

I have put a good many stories in here about people I have sat with. I did that because when I was hurting, a rule never helped me half as much as hearing about somebody who had walked the same road.

If you are reading this in the middle of the night, because you could not sleep — God is awake too. He never was asleep.

One year to change your mind. Read the whole thing. If it does not help you, write to me within a year and I will send your money back, and you keep the book. I am not going to ask you to post anything back to me or explain yourself.

It is not about getting your child back

I cannot give you that, and nobody honest can. It is about having God's peace whatever your child decides, and learning to speak so your child feels safe with you, whenever they are ready to listen.
