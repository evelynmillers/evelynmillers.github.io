Who it is for

You have somebody you have been praying for a long while now. A son who does not always call. A daughter who carries more than she tells you. Somebody whose name you say every evening without really deciding to.

I am not going to tell you this closes the distance. Nobody can promise you that, and I would not insult you by trying. What I can tell you is what thirty years of nights taught me, and what it has done to me to pray this way at my own window instead of lying there turning it over.

Thirty days of saying it properly, and not by yourself.
